Biomedical equipment service, repair, and maintenance

Hands-on technical service for medical and biomedical equipment used in healthcare, clinical, and research environments, with the documentation to show it.

Equipment service and inspection

Technical support for the equipment your teams rely on, performed in line with manufacturer requirements and your procedures.

What's included

  • Equipment inspection
  • Functional and safety checks
  • Technical diagnostics
  • Performance verification
  • Manufacturer coordination
  • Service documentation

Preventive maintenance

Scheduled maintenance programs that find small issues before they become downtime.

What's included

  • Scheduled inspections
  • Preventive maintenance procedures
  • Maintenance scheduling
  • Identification of equipment concerns
  • Service history records
  • Program reporting

Repair and troubleshooting

When equipment fails, finding the cause quickly matters. We troubleshoot on site or remotely and coordinate repairs through to completion.

What's included

  • On-site troubleshooting
  • Remote technical support
  • Fault diagnosis
  • Repair coordination
  • Parts coordination
  • Post-repair verification

Installation and commissioning

New equipment takes coordination between the manufacturer, technical staff, and users. We make sure it's set up right from day one.

What's included

  • Site readiness review
  • Installation and setup
  • Commissioning
  • Acceptance testing
  • Implementation planning
  • Post-installation support

User and technical training

Training built around the actual equipment and the environment it's used in.

What's included

  • Equipment orientation
  • Operating workflows
  • Start-up and shutdown
  • Basic troubleshooting
  • Equipment care
  • Refresher and new-user training
