End-to-end field support for EMVision technology

Everything a participating U.S. research site needs to install EMVision's investigational emu™ system and keep it performing throughout the study, delivered by one accountable team.

Installation and site activation

We plan and carry out every installation with your clinical, engineering, and IT teams so the system is ready for study start.

What's included

  • Site readiness assessment
  • Space and workflow planning
  • Delivery, receiving, and unpacking
  • System setup and installation
  • Network and IT coordination
  • Commissioning and performance verification
  • Installation documentation
  • Study start support

Clinical team training

Structured, hands-on training on the investigational system, in the research setting where it will be used.

What's included

  • Operator training per study protocol
  • Headset fitting and positioning
  • Scan workflow at the bedside
  • Start-up, shutdown, and daily checks
  • Basic troubleshooting
  • Device care and cleaning
  • New-staff onboarding
  • Refresher training

Available for stroke neurology, emergency departments, nursing, research coordinators, and clinical engineering teams, on site or remotely.

Field engineering and maintenance

Keeping systems available and performing, with fast response when something needs attention.

What's included

  • Preventive maintenance
  • On-site troubleshooting
  • Remote technical support
  • Software and firmware updates
  • Performance checks
  • Parts and repair coordination
  • Service records and history
  • Escalation to EMVision engineering

Clinical study support

Device-side support that helps investigational sites run smoothly and keeps study timelines on track.

What's included

  • Site initiation support
  • System uptime monitoring
  • Device logistics and shipping
  • Coordination with study teams
  • Technical issue tracking
  • Liaison with EMVision in Australia

Engineering and development

As EMVision's U.S. field operations partner, we help turn real-world hospital experience into better products.

What's included

  • Field feedback to R&D
  • New hardware and software rollouts
  • In-hospital testing support
  • Usability observations
  • Workflow integration insights
  • Continuous improvement reporting

Questions about our services?

Contact our team to learn more about how Vita Tech supports participating research sites.
