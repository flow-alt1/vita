What to expect when your hospital joins EMVision's research program

A clear process, minimal infrastructure, and a dedicated U.S. team supporting your clinicians from the first site visit onward.

Minimal footprint

Designed to fit into your existing workflow

Design characteristics as described by EMVision. The emu™ is an investigational device and is currently used only as part of EMVision's clinical research.

Portable

Easily moved and stored, much like a cart ultrasound.

No special infrastructure

No ionizing radiation, no ferromagnetism, and no shielding requirements.

Bedside design

Designed for scanning at the bedside, without transporting the patient.

Rapid scan design

Designed to provide neurodiagnostic output within minutes, as described by EMVision.

Designed for simple operation

One headset for all head sizes, pre-set positioning, and push-to-scan.

Supported locally

A U.S. team available on site and remotely, backed by EMVision engineering.

The process

Six steps from assessment to ongoing support

  1. Site assessment

    We review the clinical space, workflow, power, network, and IT requirements with your team.

  2. Delivery & installation

    Our engineers receive, unpack, position, and set up the system on your premises.

  3. Verification

    Commissioning and performance checks confirm the system is ready before the first study scan.

  4. Team training

    Hands-on sessions prepare operators, clinicians, and research staff to scan confidently.

  5. Study start

    We are on site for the first study scans, answering questions and resolving issues in real time.

  6. Ongoing support

    Maintenance, software updates, and remote or on-site support throughout the study.

FAQ

Common questions from hospitals

What is the emu™'s FDA status?
emu™ is an investigational device currently in clinical trials. It is being used in clinical studies at participating hospitals and, as a device still in development, is not yet FDA cleared and is not available for sale or for routine clinical care. EMVision is working toward a future FDA submission.
Does the system need a special room or shielding?
According to EMVision, emu™ has no ionizing radiation and no ferromagnetism, and needs no special infrastructure or shielding. It is portable and can be stored and moved much like a cart ultrasound.
How long does installation take?
It depends on your site, workflow, and IT requirements. We agree on a schedule with your team during the site assessment so there are no surprises on installation day.
Who trains our staff?
Vita Tech does. We provide hands-on operator training on site, followed by new-staff onboarding and refresher sessions as your team changes.
What support is available after installation?
Preventive maintenance, software updates, remote support, and on-site service, coordinated directly with EMVision's engineering team in Australia when needed.
How can our hospital get involved?
Participation in EMVision's clinical research is determined by EMVision and its study teams. Contact us and we'll connect you with the right people to discuss next steps.

Questions from a participating site?

Our team is available to answer questions about installation, training, and ongoing support.
