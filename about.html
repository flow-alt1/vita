EMVision's partner on the ground in the United States

Vita Tech LLC is the U.S. field operations partner for EMVision Medical Devices Ltd. Working under contract with EMVision from Vienna, Virginia, we support EMVision's investigational portable brain imaging technology in U.S. clinical research and development.

Who we are

A local team for a global innovator

EMVision is an Australian medical device company developing world-first portable electromagnetic brain scanners for stroke diagnosis and monitoring.

As EMVision's clinical program grows in the United States, hospitals need a partner close by. Vita Tech provides that presence: engineers who install and maintain systems, trainers who prepare clinical teams, and a direct line back to EMVision's research and development team.

We work closely with EMVision, holding ourselves to the same standards of quality, documentation, and clinical care.

Two continents, one mission

  1. Virginia, United States

    Vita Tech LLC. U.S. field operations, installation, training, and technical support.

  2. Sydney, Australia

    EMVision Medical Devices Ltd. Global headquarters, research and development, and clinical programs.

Our values

How we work with every hospital

Reliability

Research sites depend on equipment that performs every time. We are careful, thorough, and consistent in everything we install and maintain.

Responsiveness

Clinical teams can't wait on answers. We communicate clearly and act quickly when something needs attention.

Precision

Installation, verification, and documentation are done to a high standard, every time, at every site.

Partnership

We work side by side with physicians, nurses, research coordinators, clinical engineering, and IT.

Continuous learning

Every installation teaches us something. We bring those lessons back to EMVision to improve the next one.

Patients first

Behind every system is a patient who may be having a stroke. That responsibility guides how we work.
