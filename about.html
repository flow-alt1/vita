Technology supporting better healthcare

Vita Tech LLC is a Virginia-based biomedical and regulatory services company providing equipment service, maintenance, training, and regulatory support to organizations working with medical technology.

Who we are

Medical technology is only as effective as the support behind it

Healthcare organizations and medical technology companies need dependable equipment, knowledgeable users, effective maintenance, responsive support, and strong documentation.

Vita Tech works with clients to meet those needs throughout the medical device lifecycle. We start by understanding each client's equipment, environment, technical requirements, and regulatory objectives, then build a support approach around them.

Our work includes biomedical equipment service, preventive maintenance, repair, FDA registration and submission support, and regulatory compliance. We also work under contract with EMVision Medical Devices to install, maintain, and support its portable brain imaging scanner at U.S. hospitals.

Our mission

Reliable service. Practical expertise. Lasting relationships.

Our mission is to support healthcare organizations and medical technology companies with reliable biomedical services, practical technical expertise, effective training, and regulatory support.

We aim to build long-term relationships based on responsiveness, professionalism, transparency, and dependable service.
